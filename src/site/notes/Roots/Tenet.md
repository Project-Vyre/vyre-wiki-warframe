> Specific Tenet weapons are not obtainable from Sisters of Parvos and must be obtained from Ergo Glast.
> 
> # Requirements
> As they are not obtained from the Foundry, the following requirements must be met:
> - Player must be Mastery Rank 16.
> - 10 Corrupted Holokeys for each purchase.
>

> - Tenet Ferrox
> 
> ## Melee
> - Tenet Agendus
> - Tenet Exec
> - Tenet Grigori
