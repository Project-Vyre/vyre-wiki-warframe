# Rank 5
- Gain 6% Ability Strength for every 250 Max Health up to 72%.
- +1 Arcane Revive


> [!TIP] Tip
> Requires 3,000 Health to achieve 72% Ability Strength.
> 
> Usually used with [[Arcanes/Warframe Arcanes/Arcane Blessing\|Arcane Blessing]] for Warframes that don't have a lot of base Health.
