# Rank 5
On Melee kill:
+24 Health Regen/s and +660 Armor for 10s.

> [!TIP] Tip
> Can be used with Valkyr, but not exactly a required Arcane. However, it can help with achieving more efficacy with [[Arcanes/Warframe Arcanes/Arcane Battery\|Arcane Battery]] on Warframes with low base armor due to how much armor it adds.
> 
> Even then, not exactly a high priority Arcane.
