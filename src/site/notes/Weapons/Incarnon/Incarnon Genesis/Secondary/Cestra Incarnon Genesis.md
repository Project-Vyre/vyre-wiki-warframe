# Cestra Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Critical Chance increases with uninterrupted shots fired.

### Evolution `II`

#### Steadfast Grit (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +10
- On Shield/Overguard break: x3 Critical Damage for 6s

#### Fortress Salvo (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +10
- With Armor over 450: +80% Multishot

### Evolution `III`: Slayer's Nerve

> [!WARNING] Recoil on Cestra is uhhh... yeah.
> It's bad.

On Hit: +6% Accuracy and -6% Weapon Recoil for 6s. Stacks up to 10x.

### Evolution `IV`

Depends on if you want more Critical Damage or if you want more Status Chance.
#### Deathtrap Trigger (2)

Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +2x
#### Wiseman's Regard (3)

Increase Base Status Chance by +30% of current Critical Chance, up to 40%
