# Ballistica Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire cross-shaped projectiles that punch through enemies. Deals Slash damage.

### Evolution `II`: Prolific Perforation

- Increase Base Damage by +3
- On Punch Through hit: +10% Critical Chance for 3s. Stacks up to 8x.

### Evolution `III`

Either choose from increased Accuracy and Weapon Recoil or increased Projectile Speed.
#### Void's Guidance (3)

- +40% Accuracy
- -40% Weapon Recoil

#### Swift Deliverance (2)

+50% Projectile Speed

### Evolution `IV`

Options options options...

#### Critical Parallel (3)

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +12%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +1x

#### Survivor's Edge (2)

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +7%
- Increase base Status by +7% per projectile
- Increase Base Status by +28% for Incarnon Form

or...

#### Elemental Balance (1)

- Increase Base Status by +18% per projectile
- Increase Base Status by +72% for Incarnon Form
