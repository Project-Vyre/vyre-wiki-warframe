# Sicarus Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Bullet ricochet.

### Evolution `II`: Feigned Retreat

> [!WARNING] Why not King's Gambit?
> King's Gambit requires you to land Weak Point hits only or you will not have any Critical hits.

- Increase Base Damage by +40
- +40% Damage to enemies below half Health

### Evolution `III`: Void's Guidance

> [!INFO] Why not the other nodes?
> Sicarus Prime already has a fast Reload Speed and usable Base Magazine Capcity. This should be better allocated to not missing shots as it has strong vertical recoil and some spread.

- +40% Accuracy
- -40% Weapon Recoil

### Evolution `IV`: Commodore's Fortune

Increase Base Critical Chance by +14%
