# Lato Vandal Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Gain Punch Through and Ricochet

### Evolution `II`

One requires having Overshields and the other just requires a reload from empty.

#### Haven Foray (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +22
- With Overshields: Increase Base Damage by +40

#### Reified Bane

- Increase Base Damage by +12
- Reload from Empty: Increase Base Damage by +30

### Evolution `III`

Recoil management or Ammo Efficiency? Your choice.
#### Void's Guidance (1)

- +50% Accuracy
- -50% Weapon Recoil

#### Marksman's Gain

On Headshot: 20% chance for +50% ammo Efficiency for 6s

### Evolution `IV`

You ultimately have the choice between Deathtrap Trigger (2) for high Critical Chance and Critical Damage *or* GunCO with Carnage Reign (3).

#### Deathtrap Tigger (2)

- On Equip from Primary for 4s:
    - Increase Base Critical Chance by +38%
    - Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +2.6x

#### Carnage Reign (3)

+40% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target
