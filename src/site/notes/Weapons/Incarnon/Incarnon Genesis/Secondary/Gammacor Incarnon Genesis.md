# Synoid Gammacor Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

> [!INFO]
> The Incarnon Form for Gammacor is more for utility than actual damage.

Fire projectiles that pull enemies into a Cold damage explosion

### Evolution `II`

#### Infused Shots (2)

- Increase base Damage by +6
- On 50 Energy spent: Increase Base Damage by +5 for 10s. Stacks up to 4x.

#### Sage's Resolve (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +10
- With Channeled Ability active: +25% Multisot

### Evolution `IV`: Critical Parallel

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +10%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.2x
