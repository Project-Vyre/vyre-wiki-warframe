# Despair Builds
## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Gain embedding projectiles that explode with Heat damage.

### Evolution `II`

You have choices. Condition Overload or forced to use all 3 of Stalker's weapons.

#### Fatal Affliction (1)

Use this.
- Increase Base Damage by +50
- +40% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target
#### Stalker's Vendetta (2)

I really **do not** recommend this.
- Increase Base Damage by +60
- With Dread and Hate equipped:
    - Multishot consumes ammo directly from Capacity and does +100% Damage
    - +30% Multishot

### Evolution `III`: Swift Deliverance

+50% Projectile Speed

### Evolution `IV`: Critical Parallel

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +18%
- Increase base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.4x
