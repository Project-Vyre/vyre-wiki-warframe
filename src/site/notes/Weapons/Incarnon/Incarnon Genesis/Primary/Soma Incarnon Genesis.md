# Soma Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Gain increased Status Chance and Multishot while slowing Fire Rate.

### Evolution `II`: Fortress Salvo

- Increase Base Damage by +12
- With Armor over 450: +4 Punch Through

> [!FAIL] Why not Fortifying Bloodshed?
> The way it is worded implies that it requires a **Slash Status kill**, AKA the enemy you are shooting at **has** to die from the Slash bleed and not the weapon's actual damage.
### Evolution `III`: Kinetic Battle

-50% Weapon Recoil

Personally I don't like my screen shaking so much due to recoil when Soma Prime is given Fire Rate and goes BRRRRRRRRRRT and unloads its entire magazine in a few seconds when playing [[Warframes/Gauss\|Gauss]].

Also, Soma Prime is already quite accurate, so the accuracy increase from (2) Practiced Grip isn't really needed.

### Evolution `IV`:  Fatal Affliction

+40% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target.
