# Vectis Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> For activating Vectis's Incarnon Form:
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire **while not aiming down sights** transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last. There's usually 1-3 or 1-2 nodes per Evolution, and they are always arranged from left (1), middle (2), right (3).

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire slowing projectiles that explode on headshot. Projectiles have forced Cold status.

### Evolution `II`: (2) Lone Enforcer

- Increase Base Damage by +150
- +25% Multishot if no neemies are within 5m.

### Evolution `III`: Rapid Reinforcement

+60% Reload Speed

### Evolution `IV`

Critical Parallel (2) is preferred as it's reliably active over you having to headshots, but keep in mind Vectis already has +60% Headshot Damage when fully scoped in which gets increased with Deadhead (1).

#### (2) Critical Parallel

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +8%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.5x

#### (1) Deadhead

+25% Headshot Damage
