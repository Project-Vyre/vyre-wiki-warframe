# Vectis Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> For activating Vectis's Incarnon Form:
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire **while not aiming down sights** transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.
