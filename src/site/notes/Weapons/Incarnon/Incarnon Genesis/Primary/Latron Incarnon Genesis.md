# Latron Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form
Fire projectiles that explode and deal Heat damage on every bounce. 4m Radial attack range with 20% Damage Falloff.

### Evolution `II`:

One can be used for other Warframes, and the other should be used with Warframes with 1.2 Sprint Speed minimum.

#### Riddled Target (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +6
- On Puncture Status effect: +25% Multishot for 8s. Stacks up to 4x.

#### Swift Punishment (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +6
- With Sprint Speed 1.2 or higher: +30% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target.

### Evolution `III`: Marksman's Hand (1)
-60% Weapon Recoil

### Evolution `IV`

Critical Parallel (3) is usually used more often because not every enemy has an actual body part for Headshot damage and more often than not you will not be reliably hitting headshots.

#### Critical Parallel (3)

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +24%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.2x

#### Deadhead (2)

+100% Headshot Damage
