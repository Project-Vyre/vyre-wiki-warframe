# Strun Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire staggering explosive projectiles. Radial range is 4m with a Damage Falloff of 20%.

### Evolution `II`: Blazing Barrel (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +2
- On Firing: +5 Base Multishot. Stacks up to 5x.

### Evolution `III`: Rapid Reinforcement (1)

+60% Reload Speed

### Evolution `IV`

I would take Elemental Balance (1) for an absurd amount of Status Effects.

#### (1) Elemental Balance

- Increase Base Status by +11.3% **per projectile**
- Increase Base Status by +136% for Incarnon Form

#### (2) Commodore's Fortune

Increase Base Critical Chance by +12%
