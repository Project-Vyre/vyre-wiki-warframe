# Paris Prime Builds
## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Sacrifice silence for increased projectile size and Heat damage.

### Evolution `II`

One should be used generally and the other for Warframes with a Sprint Speed of 1.2 or higher.

#### (2) Guardian's Might

- Increase Base Damage by +20
- With Overshields: Increase Base Damage by +74

#### (1) Deadly Pace

- Increase Base Damage by +20
- With Sprint Speed 1.2 or higher: +80% Fire Rate

### Evolution `III`

> [!INFO]
> Now while arrows do have Punch Through, they are still a very slow moving projectile which is why I would still recommend (3) Swift Deliverance over (1) Ardent Trigger.

#### (3) Swift Deliverance

+60% Projectile Speed

#### (1) Ardent Trigger

On Punch Through hit: +40% Fire Rate for 6s

### Evolution `IV`: Striking Succession

On Hit: Increase Base Damage by +15 for 3s. Stacks up to 4x.

> [!FAIL] What about Vicious Promise?
> It requires **undamaged** enemies to take effect. Please do tell me about the possibility of some enemy remaining unscathed when there are so many abilities that have a wide coverage. No? Exactly why this is useless.
