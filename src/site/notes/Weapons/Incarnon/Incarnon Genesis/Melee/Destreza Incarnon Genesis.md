# Destreza Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE] Melee Incarnon Form Activation
> Reach 6x Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Heavy Attacks spawn ghostly rapiers.
- +100% Melee Damage
- +20% Sprint Speed
- +20% Parkour Velocity
- **+10% Puncture Damage per Heavy Attack kill, up to 300% while transformed.**

### Evolution `II`: Piercing Stature

- Increase Base Damage by +40
- On Puncture Status Effect: Gain +20% Status Chance for 6 seconds. Stacks 5x.

> [!NOTE]
> If it's not obvious... 20% stacked 5 times is 100%.


### Evolution `III`

> [!NOTE]
> Again, Tennokai mods have made Heavy Attack Efficiency largely irrelevant. Discipline's Merit is especially very potent as you are able to do a Heavy Attack every 4 hits WITHOUT consuming Combo. As such, additional Range would be more beneficial.

#### Orokin Reach (1)

+1 Range

#### Adept Reflexes (3)

+20 Initial Combo

### Evolution `IV`: Decisive Stature

**Increase Base Critical Chance by 25% of Current Status Chance, up to 35%.**
