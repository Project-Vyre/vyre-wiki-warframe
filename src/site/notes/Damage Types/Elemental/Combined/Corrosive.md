Temporarily removes up to 80% of the target's Armor value with multiple stacks.

> [!TIP] Emerald Archon Shards
> Maximum stack limit be increased through the use of Emerald Archon Shards.
> - +2 from Emerald Archon Shards
> - +3 from Tauforged Emerald Archon Shards
> 
> 100% Armor reduction is achieved with a stack limit of 14 which can be achieved with x2 Emerald Archon Shards.
> 90% Armor reduction is achieved with a stack limit of 13 which can be achieved with just **ONE** Tauforged Emerald Archon Shard.
