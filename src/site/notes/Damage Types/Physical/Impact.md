Causes the target to flail backwards temporarily, with repeated stacks increasing the size and duration of the stagger.

For heavy units such as Eximus, it also lowers the Parazon Mercy Kill threshold to 80% of total Health with multiple stacks.

>[!TIP] Mercy Kill Threshold Indicator
>The indicator in-game looks like a golden bracket on the target's Health bar.

# Interactions
- [[Weapons/Requiem/Coda/Primary/Coda Bassocyst\|Coda Bassocyst]]'s Alternate Fire which forces Impact Status effects allows it to perform a Mercy Kill at a high Health threshold for easier activation of its Unique Trait.
